# Online Bookshelf Context

## Product Language
- A **book** is a curated collection on the bookshelf, such as "AI Basics" or "Research Methods".
- A **section** is a table-of-contents group inside one book.
- A **resource** is a canonical saved item, usually a URL with title, source type, and notes.
- A **placement** puts a resource into a specific section of a specific book.
- A **tag** is a reusable label on a resource.

## MVP Shape
The MVP is single-user and local-first. Users manually create books, sections, resources, placements, and tags. One resource can appear in multiple books or sections through placements.

## Deferred Capabilities
- Authentication and multi-user ownership.
- AI categorization and summarization.
- URL metadata scraping.
- Browser extension or share-sheet ingestion.
- Hosted deployment and Postgres.
